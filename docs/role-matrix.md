### Role Matrix

- **SuperAdmin**
  - Platform owner – manages organizations, plans, audits, global dashboard.
  - Endpoints under `/api/superadmin/…`.

- **Admin**
  - Organization admin – manages users, teams, leads, pipelines, reports, dashboard.
  - Endpoints under `/api/admin/…`.

- **HR**
  - HR role – manages candidates, interviews, onboarding, HR dashboard.
  - Endpoints under `/api/hr/…`.

- **Assignee**
  - Sales / execution – manages leads, tasks, activities, personal dashboard.
  - Endpoints under `/api/assignee/…`.
